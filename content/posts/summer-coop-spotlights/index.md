Formula uOttawa gives members opportunities to work on real engineering projects,
learn from one another and take a design from an idea to a working component.
In these interviews, three Mechanical Engineering students describe their co-op
work and the team experience they brought with them.

## Meet the members

- [Jacob Thomas](#member-jacob) - Aero Lead; Nokia
- [Benjamin Marceau (Benji)](#member-benjamin-marceau) - Chassis Lead; Remstar Racing
- [Maire Levin](#member-maire-levin) - Team Captain; BRP's Sea-Doo R&D team

> **Did Formula come up in your co-op interviews?**
>
> They really wanted to know about my experience and the projects I'd worked on.
> Everyone has school experience, but not everyone does Formula, so I think
> that helped me stand out.

> **What did you learn with the team?**
>
> I helped with most of our carbon fibre layups and did a lot of CFD work on
> the car. Preparing for the design event taught me to explain my reasoning,
> rather than just repeat facts.

> **What are you working on at Nokia?**
>
> One problem we're trying to solve is parts warping after long exposure to
> heat. The product has tight tolerances, so even a small change in shape
> can be a big issue.

> **What if someone feels they don't know enough to join?**
>
> You're not going to learn about the car until you start getting involved.
> Everyone's more than happy to explain things. You just need to be willing
> to learn.

> **What do you work on at Remstar Racing?**
>
> After the car runs, I analyze data such as temperature, sensor readings,
> and voltage. I also work with drivers to review specific moments on the
> track. I design tools to help us analyze data more quickly and improve
> efficiency and performance.

> **What skills have you developed with the team?**
>
> I've learned the importance of teamwork and paying attention to small
> details, how to work on race cars, and how to manage tight deadlines when
> things get busy. I've also learned how to accept criticism and use
> feedback to improve.

> **How did Formula come up in your co-op interviews?**
>
> They wanted to know what I had worked on for the car. They asked whether
> I was only involved in design or if I also had hands-on experience. They
> also wanted to know what I had learned from being part of the team.

> **Do you need experience before joining the team?**
>
> You should join anyway! Nobody knows everything when they first start.
> If you're willing to learn, we're always happy to teach you. Everyone
> is welcome, and we try to find a project for everyone to get involved in.

> **What did you work on with BRP's Sea-Doo R&D team?**
>
> I carried out iterative finite element analysis (FEA) on a new fishing
> boat assembly, then took responsibility for ordering, obtaining and
> assembling the first prototypes.

> **What has completing a full design cycle taught you?**
>
> The most valuable thing I have learned is participating in the full
> design process. Going from research to design, design reviews,
> iterative simulation, manufacturing, and testing. I have been able
> to complete multiple full design cycles for different components
> on the race car.

> **How did your Formula experience come up in interviews?**
>
> Once they see I've been doing Formula for a few years, the conversation
> always gets steered there.

> **Can you join without knowing about race cars?**
>
> I changed degrees for this club because I didn't know I liked race cars
> until I saw it. I knew nothing about race cars, and now I am Team Captain.
> I still don't know everything, but we all love to learn and share
> what we know.
